CCM Generator

Enter the syllabus, number of lectures and slot. Get a CALTech Course Manual (.docx) with a session-by-session plan. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

0 Start from the syllabus PDF

Upload the course syllabus (one PDF). The app reads the course code, title, objectives, outcomes, units with hours, books and suggested experiments and fills the form below. Check everything afterwards; PDFs differ and the unit → CO links are a best guess.
